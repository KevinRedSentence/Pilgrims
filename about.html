About Pilgrims

An independent coffee shop built on good coffee, good company and a love of Woking.

Where it began

A resting place for travellers of every kind

Woking sits on the old routes through Surrey, and for centuries people have passed through on their way somewhere else. We named ourselves Pilgrims in that spirit — a welcoming place to stop, refuel and carry on refreshed.

Today our travellers are commuters catching the early train, parents after the school run, friends catching up and anyone who needs a quiet hour with a good book. Whatever brings you in, there's a seat and a cup waiting for you.

Our promise

Great coffee shouldn't be complicated. We keep things simple: quality ingredients, skilled hands and genuine hospitality, every single day.
